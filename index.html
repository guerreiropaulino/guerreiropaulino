About

Who gets in, who stays, and why it matters where.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Department of Geography of the University of Zurich. My work asks how institutional conditions at Swiss Gymnasia shape the educational pathways of minoritised students.

I studied Educational Science, Philosophy and Social Data Science at the University of Zurich. Before starting my PhD, I worked on research projects at teacher education universities and on European education and research policy in Brussels.

Research

Institutional conditions for minoritised students at Swiss Gymnasia

My dissertation is part of a project funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). It examines how Gymnasia, as institutions, create or limit opportunities for students from minoritised backgrounds.
